# ResearchProof AI 1.0 — Phase 5 Evidence Validation & Research Integrity
## Verification of Academic Extraction, Grounding & Anti-Hallucination Guardrails

### 1. Executive Integrity Summary
The Literature Review Studio in ResearchProof AI 1.0 is engineered with an uncompromising anti-hallucination architecture. In academic publishing, fabricating empirical metrics, statistical estimates, study participants, or bibliographical metadata represents severe scientific misconduct. Phase 5 strictly enforces verifiable provenance across all extraction, synthesis, and comparison pipelines.

---

### 2. Evidence Grounding Architecture

```
[ Academic Source Abstract / Full-Text ]
                    │
                    ▼
[ Deterministic Provenance Parser ]
  ├─ Exact sample size matching (`N = \d+`, `\d+ participants`)
  ├─ Direct quote extraction with character offsets
  ├─ Inferred study design (evidence-backed keyword classifier)
  └─ Statistical reporting validation (F, t, p, d, r, CI)
                    │
                    ▼
[ EvidenceExtract Provenance Record ]
  ├─ Verbatim sourceText snippet stored in DB
  ├─ Section / Page provenance attribution
  ├─ Review confidence status: 'pending_review' | 'verified'
  └─ Linked directly to parent LiteratureEntry & Document
                    │
                    ▼
[ Evidence-Linked Synthesis Engine ]
  ├─ Every paragraph strictly attributed via APA 7 in-text citations
  ├─ No claims generated without a supporting `studyId` in evidenceLinks
  └─ Full bibliography automatically generated from verified metadata
```

### 4. Candidate Research Gap Integrity Model
Research gaps are systematically deduced from observable limitations and methodological concentrations across the analyzed corpus:

1. **Methodological Gaps:** Triggered when reviewed literature is dominated by cross-sectional/observational designs lacking causal intervention.
2. **Population & Sampling Gaps:** Triggered when reviewed cohorts are concentrated in homogeneous Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, and Democratic (WEIRD) or university convenience samples.
3. **Measurement & Instrument Gaps:** Triggered when studies rely exclusively on subjective self-report questionnaires rather than objective physiological or behavioral metrics.
4. **Effect Size Heterogeneity & Contradictory Evidence:** Triggered when empirical effect sizes display variance across institutional contexts, indicating unmodeled moderators.

Every candidate gap generated by the engine includes:
- Grounded `evidenceBasis` summarizing the literature pattern.
- Explicit list of `supportingStudyIds` linking back to the verified corpus.
- Concrete, actionable future `researchQuestions` suitable for dissertation or grant proposals.

---

### 5. Multi-Study Comparison Verification
The side-by-side comparison engine evaluates up to 5 studies concurrently:
- **Consensus Analysis:** Computes actual convergence across study designs, participant scales, and replicated empirical effects.
- **Divergence Analysis:** Flags discrepancies in instrumentation, divergent statistical methodologies, and conflicting findings.
- **Zero Fabrication:** If an empirical dimension was not extracted or reported in a study, the comparison grid explicitly renders `Not reported` or `—` rather than guessing.

---

### 6. Audit Trail & Reproducibility
- All evidence extractions generate immutable `EvidenceExtract` documents with creation timestamps and user attribution.
- Literature entries maintain an `extractionStatus` state machine:
  $$\text{pending} \longrightarrow \text{in\_progress} \longrightarrow \text{completed} \longrightarrow \text{verified}$$
- Reviewers and investigators can inspect the exact source abstract snippet that justified every extracted data cell.
